Consent to Record and Use Your Voice

Version 1.0

Your Voice and Privacy

Your voice recordings are personal information. Project X / Sammy Dean Productions uses them to create an AI singing voice profile for songs you request. This V1.0 notice explains your choices. You must be 18 or older, signed in to your own account, and recording your own voice.

Your current order

Your recordings may include individual takes, combined training audio, and an optional spoken message. We create a Tier 2 voice profile and working audio to produce your current song. The optional spoken message is excluded from voice-model training. We associate these records with your account email, recording session, profile and order. We also save the recording and listening setup you select, plus available microphone information for quality review and support. We do not collect persistent device IDs.

Choose whether to keep your profile

Choose either deletion after this order or storage for future purchases. If you choose deletion after this order, current-order recordings, the voice profile and related working files are scheduled for active deletion after the seven-calendar-day support period following delivery of your finished song by email. If you report a problem during that period, we retain only the files needed to resolve it and then remove those files from active storage.

Keeping your profile for future gifts

If you choose storage for future purchases, we keep your verified recordings, Tier 2 voice profile, calibration and linked setup information for 90 calendar days from accepting consent. We email the account address three calendar days before the scheduled deletion date with a direct link to read and re-sign the consent form. If you have not re-signed, we send a second, last-chance email one calendar day before deletion with the same direct consent link. Re-signing before the deadline starts a fresh 90-day period from re-signing and preserves the existing profile and linked data. You do not need to re-record or retrain your voice. Without renewal, the profile becomes unavailable at the deadline and its associated voice data is scheduled for deletion from active systems and the local master library. Expiry is calculated in the America/Halifax time zone; the reminder shows the exact deadline. Withdrawal, a deletion request or account closure may end storage earlier. Finished songs retain their separate seven-day support period.

Optional improvement permission

You may separately permit internal development, testing and evaluation of voice capture and singing conversion, including listening to samples and comparing results. Participation is voluntary and unpaid. Declining does not affect your order or your storage choice. Improvement use is allowed only while your profile has an active storage permission and you have selected this optional permission. Re-signing the storage consent does not automatically select improvement use; you choose again on the renewal form. Improvement permission never extends the storage deadline. If you withdraw improvement permission, further improvement use stops; copies retained solely for improvement are scheduled for active deletion. Copies also needed for your separately authorized order or renewable profile storage follow that permission.

Where files are handled

Recordings are captured through a service hosted on Render and transferred to Project X’s controlled workstation library. After verifying a transfer, we request removal of temporary Render-side audio. This does not itself verify deletion from backups.

Backups and deletion

Active deletion may leave recoverable backup copies. Their availability and expiry depend on the storage provider, account settings and any applicable hold. We explain the backup limits that apply when confirming a deletion request. We do not use backup copies for production or improvement after the relevant permission ends. Restoring a backup must reapply current withdrawal and deletion records before voice data can be used again.

Change your choices or request deletion

Email sdp.projectx.contact@gmail.com using the email associated with your Project X account and include your order or recording-session reference. Do not attach voice recordings. We verify the request and identify the recordings, voice profile, working files and improvement copies it covers. We confirm which active copies have been removed and explain remaining backup limits. Sending a request does not confirm that deletion is complete. Renewing permission cannot restore data already deleted or override a confirmed withdrawal or deletion request.

Separate consent records

We keep a separate record of the document version, choices and acceptance or withdrawal history so we can establish what was authorized. Updating this notice does not rewrite your earlier consent evidence or silently grant new permissions.

Limits on use

Permission for an order, future purchases or internal improvement does not authorize unrelated uses, public release of your voice by Project X or training a general-purpose AI model. Those uses require separate information and permission.

One place to give consent

Read these terms and choose your permissions in the voice recorder before recording. The public privacy page explains the same choices; it does not require a second consent submission.
