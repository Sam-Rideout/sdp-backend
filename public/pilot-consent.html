Recording workflow test notice

This is a limited test for invited helpers. It is not a customer order. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop before starting a session.

What the recordings are for

Your eight-section recording will be used only to test the recording workflow and evaluate capture quality. It will not be used in customer orders or production machine-learning training. This pilot does not grant permission for public release or unrelated uses.

Where recordings are stored

The website temporarily stores the separate recording clips on Render while they are transferred to Sam Rideout’s PC. After the local transfer is verified, the live Render audio files are deleted. The verified local pilot copy is stored in the Project X voice library under a separate pilot folder.

Render creates encrypted daily snapshots of its persistent disk. Render documents that snapshots are retained for at least 7 days and does not publish a maximum retention period. Deleting the live Render files therefore does not guarantee immediate removal of those files from existing snapshots. Snapshot expiry is controlled by Render.

How long the pilot copy is kept

The active local pilot copy is kept while the limited pilot is active. Sam will remove it when the pilot ends, unless you separately agree to another use. You may ask for deletion sooner. Because Render snapshots have no published maximum retention period, we cannot promise an exact date when every backup copy expires.

Your choice and requests

Do not record another person’s voice or include private information. You may stop participating or request deletion by emailing sam.rideout.me@gmail.com. Sam Rideout, President of SDP Sammy Dean Productions, is responsible for handling pilot data and requests.
