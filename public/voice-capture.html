Voice recording session

This guided recording takes about 10–20 minutes; one pass is about 10 minutes. For a stronger, more useful voice profile, repeat at least three numbered sections twice. You can repeat any section more than twice. Use a quiet room and keep a steady distance from your microphone. Record your voice by itself: no backing music, reverb, pitch correction, compression, or doubling. Sing comfortably without straining. Mistakes are fine: pause, breathe, and repeat the phrase. Each take is saved separately, so you can review and redo a section without overwriting an earlier take.
