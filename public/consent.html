DRAFT PREVIEW — NOT LIVE OR OPERATIONAL

Consent to record and use your voice

Organization: Project X / Sammy Dean Productions

Eligibility: This draft is for adults aged 18 or older. A verified age-confirmation step must be in place before customer recording is enabled.

What this consent covers

Project X records separate voice takes to create and use an AI singing voice profile for your current song-conversion order. Each take remains available as a separate recording. A combined audio file, if created, is a derived working copy.

To start recording, you must choose exactly one of the two required retention options on the recording page. Both options include permission to record and use your voice for the current order. Neither option is selected in advance. Development use is a separate, optional choice.

Where recordings are stored

During capture, recordings are temporarily stored by the Render-hosted capture service. They are transferred to Project X’s controlled PC library. The service removes its audio copies after the transfer has been verified. The PC library and working folders hold the separate takes and the voice profile according to the retention option you select.

Your required retention choice

Delete after this order. Project X will remove the recordings and voice profile from active systems and the PC master library within three calendar days after the current order is complete. This brief period allows current-order technical issues to be resolved. It does not permit future-order or development use.
Keep for future purchases. Project X will retain the recordings and voice profile for your future song-conversion purchases until you withdraw this permission or request deletion.

Backup retention and expiry details: [to be supplied and disclosed before customer launch].

Optional development use

You may separately allow Project X to use recordings and the voice profile for internal development, testing, and evaluation of its voice-capture and singing-conversion process. This permission is optional and does not affect the current order. It does not authorize public release of your voice or unrelated uses.

Development access, service-provider access, and retention details: [to be completed before customer launch].

Withdrawal and deletion requests

You may ask Project X to stop future-order or development use and delete linked voice data. For testing, send a request to sam.rideout.me@gmail.com. Include the email address on your account and your order or capture-session reference. Do not email audio recordings.

Test contact only: this mailbox is not connected to an automated deletion workflow. Sending a request does not itself confirm that data has been deleted. A verified request-handling process must be tested before real customer use.

A request will cover active service copies, the PC master library, working copies, profiles, and development copies where applicable. Any backup copies that cannot be individually removed will follow the backup expiry schedule once it is established and disclosed. The deletion request policy and timing must be operationally tested before customer launch.

Before customer use

This consent is a draft and does not authorize a live customer recording session. Project X must complete the bracketed retention and development details, verify the deletion process across all data locations, and publish a working request contact before inviting customers.
