PROJECT X · VOICE CAPTURE

DRAFT PREVIEW — CUSTOMER CAPTURE NOT ENABLED

Consent to record and use your voice

Organization: Project X / Sammy Dean Productions

Eligibility: This draft is for adults aged 18 or older recording their own voice. Before starting a customer session, you must confirm both statements on the recording page. This is a self-declaration.

What this consent covers

Project X records separate voice takes to train an AI singing voice model, create a voice profile, and use it for your current song-conversion order. Each take remains available as a separate recording. A combined audio file, if created, is a derived working copy.

To start recording, you must choose exactly one of the two required retention options on the recording page. Both options include permission to record and use your voice for the current order. Neither option is selected in advance. Development use is a separate, optional choice.

Where recordings are stored

During capture, recordings are temporarily stored by the Render-hosted capture service. They are transferred to Project X’s controlled PC library. After the local transfer has been verified, the service deletes the audio files from Render’s active storage. The PC library and working folders hold the separate takes, derived working audio, and voice profile according to the retention option you select. Deletion from active storage does not confirm deletion from snapshots or backups.

Your required retention choice

Delete after this order. Project X will remove the recordings, derived working audio, and voice profile from active storage, including the PC master library and working folders, within three calendar days after the current order is complete. This brief period allows current-order technical issues to be resolved. This option does not authorize future-order use. Optional development use, if selected separately, ends when this retention period ends and does not delay deletion. The three-day deadline does not apply to snapshots or backups.
Keep for future purchases. Project X will retain the recordings and voice profile for your future song-conversion purchases until you withdraw this permission or request deletion.

Snapshots and backups: Render documents encrypted daily persistent-disk snapshots that are available for at least seven days after creation. A recording present when a snapshot is taken may be included in it. Deleting the active audio file does not establish that its snapshot copies have been deleted. Render has not confirmed a maximum retention period or permanent-purge schedule. Project X therefore cannot promise when every snapshot copy will be removed. Any other backup retention details remain to be confirmed before customer launch.

Optional development use

You may separately allow Project X to use recordings and the voice profile for internal development, testing, and evaluation of its voice-capture and singing-conversion process while they are retained under your selected option. This permission is optional and does not affect the current order. It does not extend retention, delay deletion, or authorize public release of your voice or unrelated uses.

Project X performs voice-model training and internal testing on its controlled local PC. Render hosts the temporary capture service and its disk snapshots. Any additional development access or service-provider arrangements must be disclosed before use.

Withdrawal and deletion requests

You may ask Project X to stop future-order or development use and delete linked voice data. For testing, send a request to sam.rideout.me@gmail.com. Include the email address on your account and your order or capture-session reference. Do not email audio recordings.

Test contact only: this mailbox is not connected to an automated deletion workflow. Sending a request does not itself confirm that data has been deleted. A verified request-handling process must be tested before real customer use.

A request will cover active service copies, the PC master library, working copies, profiles, and development copies where applicable. Deletion confirmations will identify the active-storage locations checked and will not claim that all snapshot or backup copies are gone. Permanent deletion timing for Render snapshot copies remains unconfirmed. The deletion request policy and timing must be operationally tested before customer launch.

Before customer use

This consent is a draft and does not authorize a live customer recording session. Before enabling customer capture, Project X must test the required age-and-voice declaration and the deletion-request process, test the chosen active-storage deletion deadlines across the PC master library and working folders, and confirm any additional backup or provider arrangements. The wording above does not itself enable or verify these processes.

This document describes consent and data use. Recording prompts and recording procedures are provided separately.
